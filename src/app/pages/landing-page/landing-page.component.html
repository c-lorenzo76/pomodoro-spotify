Hero Section

Why Pomodoro?

A time management technique invented by Francesco Cirillo. Designed to maximizing productivity and preventing burnout by breaking work into intervals. The intervals are called pomodoros, which comes from the Italian word for "tomato". I don't know why I included this but now you know and if you already knew fuck off.

About Apple Music - Pomodoro

Now this is a service that uses the pomodoro technique while listening to music. I chose Apple Music as the music provider as it's the one I use and having seen other similar services using Spotify I decided to create one for Apple enthusiasts.

Apple Music - Pomodoro is minimalistic but I will continue to add onto the amount features. Currently the features I will be focusing on is:

  • more configuration (focus time, break time, number of sessions etc),
  • choosing playlists for focus/break time,
  • statistics
  • dark mode
  • many more....
