Why Pomodoro?

A time management technique invented by Francesco Cirillo. Designed to maximizing productivity and preventing burnout by breaking work into intervals. The intervals are called pomodoros, which comes from the Italian word for "tomato".

About 25/5

This is a service that uses the pomodoro technique while listening to music.

25/5 is minimalistic but I will continue to add onto the features. Currently the features I will be focusing on is:

  • more configuration (focus time, break time, number of sessions etc)

  • choosing playlists for focus/break time

  • statistics

Frequently Asked Questions

Why do I have to log into Spotify?

Logging into Spotify allows 25/5 to access your playlists and play music on your behalf. This is necessary for the app to function as intended, as it relies on Spotify's music streaming capabilities.

Will 25/5 be able to track my data?

The app will not track your personal data or usage patterns. It only uses the Spotify API to access your playlists and play music. Your privacy is important, and the app is designed to respect that.

What if I don't want to log in?

The app requires a Spotify login to function properly. If you choose not to log in, you will not be able to use the music playback features.
